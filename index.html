About STACKLY

We Believe Every Action Can Change a Life

Born in 2014 from a weekend food drive, STACKLY has grown into a movement of 5,000+ volunteers working across education, healthcare, environment and community development.

  • Registered 80G & 12A non-profit, audited annually
  • Programs in 120+ villages and urban settlements
  • 92% of donations reach the field directly
